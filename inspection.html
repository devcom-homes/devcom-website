Unit Inspection

Read all instructions below before starting. Complete every section in order before obtaining signatures.

How to use this form
1
Select the inspection type below — Move-In or Move-Out — then tap Continue.
2
Fill out each section and tap Next to advance. You can tap Back at any time to return to a previous section. Do not close or refresh the page — your progress will be lost.
3
In the Room Inspection section, tap Good, Fair, Poor, or N/A for each room. Check off each item in the list as you inspect it. Use the Notes field to describe any issues. Tap Attach Photos to add pictures from your phone — take photos of anything that is not in Good condition.
4
In the Systems and Safety sections, tap Pass, Fail, or N/A for each item as you test it. Do not guess — physically test each one before tapping.
5
The Damage Log fills in automatically based on your room ratings and notes. When you reach that section, review each item, correct any descriptions that need more detail, and remove anything that does not apply. Tap + Add Item if anything was missed.
6
On the Signatures page, the tenant signs first using their finger on the screen, then types their name and taps Done. You then sign the same way. Once both signatures are complete, tap Submit Inspection Report.
7
Wait for the confirmation screen before leaving. It will show a reference number confirming the report was sent. If you do not see the confirmation screen, the report was not submitted.
Do not close this page until you see the confirmation screen. The report is not saved on your device — it is only saved once submitted.
