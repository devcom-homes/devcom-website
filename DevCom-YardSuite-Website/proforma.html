How Does ADU Financing Work?

Most homeowners can fund an ADU build using equity they already have — they just don't know which route fits their situation. This tool helps you understand the return on your investment and compare the most common financing paths before speaking with a broker.

How to use this tool

1

Check your ROI

Enter your build cost and expected rent to see if the numbers make sense before you go further.

2

Compare financing

Pick the scenario that matches your situation and plug in your real numbers. Results update as you type.

3

Talk to TMC

Bring your numbers to TMC Greater Simcoe. They'll verify qualification, rates, and walk you through next steps.

Key terms

LTV — Loan-to-Value ratio. The mortgage as a percentage of property value.
As-Complete Value — What the property will be worth after the ADU is built, verified by appraiser.
CMHC Premium — Mortgage insurance required when LTV exceeds 80%. Added to the loan.
Equity Available — Cash you can access by refinancing, after paying off your existing mortgage.
