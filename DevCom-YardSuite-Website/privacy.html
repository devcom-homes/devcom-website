Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

1. Who we are

YardSuite is an Ontario-based ADU planning platform operated in partnership with DevCom Homes. We help homeowners assess, finance, and build garden suites and other accessory dwelling units.

2. What we collect

When you use YardSuite, we may collect: your name, email, phone number, property address, lot size estimates, intended use, financing preferences, and any surveys or documents you voluntarily upload. We also collect limited technical data (IP address, browser type, device) through standard web analytics.

3. How we use it

We use your information to provide the planning tools, match you with DevCom Homes or a vetted regional partner builder, and facilitate introductions to financing partners such as TMC Greater Simcoe. We do not sell your data.

4. Who we share it with

Your project details are shared only with (a) DevCom Homes, (b) the regional partner builder matched to your property location, and (c) TMC Greater Simcoe if you elect to explore financing. We require all partners to handle your information confidentially.

5. Your rights (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing privacy@yardsuite.ca.

6. Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and local storage to remember your calculator inputs and improve your experience. We use aggregated analytics to understand usage patterns. No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers.

7. Contact

Questions? Email privacy@yardsuite.ca.
