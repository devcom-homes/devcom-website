Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Who we are

YardSuite is an Ontario-based ADU planning platform operated in partnership with DevCom Homes. We help homeowners assess, finance, and build garden suites and other accessory dwelling units.

2. What we collect

When you use YardSuite, we may collect: your name, email, phone number, property address, lot size estimates, intended use, financing preferences, and any surveys or documents you voluntarily upload. We also collect limited technical data (IP address, browser type, device) through standard web analytics.

3. How we use it

We use your information to provide the planning tools, connect you with DevCom Homes, and facilitate introductions to financing partners such as TMC Greater Simcoe. We do not sell your data.

4. Who we share it with

Your project details are shared only with (a) DevCom Homes and (b) TMC Greater Simcoe if you choose to explore financing. We require them to handle your information confidentially. We also use service providers to run the site: forms you submit are stored by Netlify (our web host, in the United States) and may be copied to a DevCom Google Workspace spreadsheet; when you look up an address, it is sent to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and Overpass, for nearby building outlines) and, for Collingwood, the Town's public map service to find your lot; satellite images come from Esri. Backyard photos you add to the Design Studio stay on your device and are never uploaded.

5. Your rights (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing info@devcomhomes.ca.

6. Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and local storage to remember your calculator inputs and improve your experience. We use aggregated analytics to understand usage patterns. No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers.

7. Contact

Questions? Email info@devcomhomes.ca.
